AAN Gen 11 — living design system extracted from the running product

The system, as shipped

Every component below is the product's own DOM, pulled out of the ten live Gen 11 pages and rendered by the product's own stylesheets. Nothing here is drawn for the catalogue. If a specimen and the page disagree, the specimen is wrong and gets regenerated — _tools/gen-specimens.js rebuilds them all from source.

Each chapter is one page's stylesheet stack, loaded in a frame so that ten stacks can coexist without colliding. Under every specimen: the selector, the ancestor chain its CSS is scoped to, the files that style it, and its measured geometry at 1440×900.
