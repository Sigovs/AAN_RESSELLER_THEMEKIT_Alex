Rules and backend map

Every component of the AAN control panel, in the order it is built: tokens make atoms, atoms make molecules, molecules make organisms, organisms make page templates, templates become pages. Each card shows the Gen 11 reference as drawn on its origin page, next to the same component on the new backend's own ui-* markup with the two package layers applied. If the two sides differ, the package is wrong, not the page.

Main document: design-system.html — every token in both themes and every component in light or dark. This page adds the rules, the solved problems and the backend side by side.

The one rule: a level is built only from the levels below it and never restyles them. A table does not change how a button looks; a page does not change how a table looks.

Rules DESIGN.md §0–42 · three review rounds · the cross-page audit

The components below are what the system looks like. These rules are why it looks like that. Each one came from a problem we measured on the ten pages and fixed; the second list names those problems so nobody brings them back.

01One system; the earlier page wins

When two pages disagree, the page made earlier wins: All Vehicles → Single Vehicle → All Leads → Single Lead → Dealer Login → My Work Queue → Single Ticket → Manage Dealers → Dealer Edit → Accounting. A page never restyles a shared component.

Why
Without an order, every page invented its own version: 7 button heights and 8 button radii on one page.
Check
ds-check.mjs reports no new page rule against a shared component.

02Nothing jumps

No state change moves anything: hover, select, open, save, error, loading. Reserve the space ahead of time. A chip is as wide as its longest state, a result line is always there, and an error replaces the help line in the same place.

Why
The rule Alex raised most often across three rounds.
Check
Measure the shift. It must be 0px.

03Attached or floating, never both

Decide every boundary first. Attached: square corners where surfaces touch, one hairline, no shadow between them, the same inset. Floating: a gap on every side, its own full radius and one shadow.

Why
The sticky stack read as four stacked rounded panels.
Check
No radius on a touching edge; no two shadows on one boundary.

04Radius from the scale; the pill is semantic

4 micro · 5 checkbox · 7 inner (fields, chips, menus) · 9 control (buttons, facets) · 12 panel and bar · 14 card. The pill is only for segmented tracks, flag tags, counters, switches, avatars and the id pill.

Why
One page had 19 different radii, from 2 to 28.
Check
Every radius on the page is a scale value; a button, field, facet, tab strip or save bar is never a pill.

05One shadow per floating object

Resting controls --sh-pill; bar, field sheet, record head and in-page popovers --sh-soft; the dock --el-2; bar menus and dialogs --el-3; the dark board its own. The engaged sticky stack casts one shadow, at its bottom edge.

Why
The product shipped 1 shadow per page; the first redesign had 18.
Check
Shadows inside the stack: 0.

06Nothing touches an edge

Text never touches the edge of the element it sits in: at least 8px each side inside any tag, chip, badge, key, menu item or nav item; at least 10px inside a button; at least 16px beside a divider or a card edge. A hairline is box-shadow: inset, never a border, on anything inside the sticky stack.

Why
Fields sat flush on a column hairline because a stronger reset had zeroed their padding. Alex: "no sticking anywhere".
Check
Measure the air between every text run and the edge of its box, and on both sides of every divider.

07Sentence case; caps only for keys

Titles, chips, tags, buttons, labels and menu items are sentence case. Caps (11px / 700, +0.08em) appear only on table head keys, spec-plate keys and menu group labels.

Why
"NO PRICE" shouts; "No price" states. Caps hide word shape.
Check
Search the CSS for uppercase: only those three places.

08One primary; red only twice

One filled brand button per view. Red appears only on Hidden on site and Clear all. A destructive action is danger text at the far end of its group and asks first. It is never a red fill.

Why
Seven pill buttons, each 275px wide, competed in one action row.
Check
Count the filled buttons and the red elements per view.

09Left-aligned; labels on one line

Every table column is left-aligned, Price included, and its head sits level with its content. A field label never wraps: widen the cell, don't break the label.

Why
A two-line label drops its input below its neighbours, and on My Work Queue all 16 column heads wrapped at 1440.
Check
Every label and head has one line box at every width.

10State facts; don't explain, don't grade

Neutral flags ("No price"), never grades ("health", "needs work", a green "OK"). Dealer pages carry no explanatory paragraphs; a rule worth knowing goes into a "?" at its label. Internal names and codes appear only in title.

Why
Scott asked seven times to remove the explanations.
Check
No sentence on a dealer page that the original doesn't have.

11Nothing is dropped on tablet

768–1279 is designed; 1280 and up stays pixel-identical. What doesn't fit wraps, reflows or scrolls inside its own container. No display: none to make room and no smaller type. The page never scrolls sideways.

Why
Never lose a feature.
Check
features.mjs: every item reachable at every tablet width.

12Focus is an outline

outline: 2px solid brand; outline-offset: 2px on :focus-visible (#ffd98a on dark surfaces). Focus owns the outline and nothing else uses it. Never draw focus with box-shadow, and never clip it with overflow: hidden.

Why
Keyboard users must see where they are on a dense page.
Check
Tab through the page; every stop shows the ring in full.

13Fake data says so

Data that is fabricated or not connected carries a Demo tag (violet, sentence case) before the reader has to decide whether to believe it. A control the original lacks carries Mock addition.

Why
A reviewer must not mistake a mock figure for a real one.
Check
Every unconnected region is tagged at its header.

14Dark is re-solved, not inverted

Neutral graphite for everything you work on, the board alone in navy — two materials, as in light. The ground is #191b1f, not black; the type is a soft near-white, never pure white. A token keeps its role, not its lightness: the well lifts in dark, hover lightens. Paper stays paper (the window sticker, the description editor's text).

Why
The first dark pass was "heavy, everything in a heap": too dark, too white, too few steps (Alex, Nov 17).
Check
dark-audit.mjs on every page: no light surface left, no text under AA on the composited render. DESIGN.md §42.

Tokens in the design system

Colour in light and dark, type, spacing, radius, elevation, icons, focus and motion, and layout live in the design system, each with the backend token that carries it. The backend receives them from one file, aan-gen11-theme.css, loaded after its own theme.css; it changes no markup, and every component that reads those variables changes at once.

Use for

  • An action on this page: Save, Export, Add vehicle.
  • The way back on a record page: the quiet small button "‹ Back to …", as a link.

Not for

  • Going to another page from running text: use a link.
  • Picking a value: use a facet, select or segmented control.

Do

  • One primary per view. Every other button is secondary or ghost.
  • Size it by its label, 8px between neighbours, padding 14 (small 11). Text never touches the edge.
  • Sentence case, three words or fewer.
  • Destructive: danger text at the far end of its group, confirm first.
  • Icon-only: the square of its tier, with aria-label and title.

Don't

  • A second filled button, a pill, or a red fill.
  • A button that changes width when its label changes.
  • More than three heights on a page: 36 default · 30 small · 44 large (login only).

States

  • Hover: primary turns --brand-hover; secondary and ghost take --wash.
  • Focus: 2px brand outline, offset 2.
  • Disabled: no shadow, label at 50%.
  • Loading: disabled, label unchanged.
  • Toggle on: brand-soft, 1px #cddbfb ring, 13.5 / 600.
  • Result ("Saved"): 16px to the left of the button; the button does not move.

Access and tablet

  • A real <button type="button">; navigation is an <a>.
  • Touch: at least 36px tall; nothing waits for hover.

Anatomy

  • Label above, 3–6px gap; then the control; then the help line.
  • An error replaces the help line in the same place.

Use for

  • Every editable value in a form, a dock or a dialog.

Do

  • Label 13 / 600 ink-2, sentence case, nowrap.
  • Help line 13 / 1.45 ink-3, only where the original has the same line.

  • Focus, disabled, missing, invalid and not-set: as the text field.
  • Saving never folds or moves the form.

Access and tablet

  • <label for>, or the label wraps the control.
  • The "?" mark is focusable (tabindex="0"); its text is in title and aria-label; it opens on tap.

Feeds Incoming

When one record owns several lines (a dealer with several feeds), the record's identity and its record-level actions span all its lines; each line carries its own cells and actions; a hairline separates the lines inside the record, a stronger line separates records. No nested tables, no expanders.

Pages the references on real data

The ten Gen 11 pages are the standard. Two more were never drawn and are not references: Feeds Incoming and Create A Ticket were built only from this system as experiments. Feeds Incoming holds up as a list. Create A Ticket does not hold up as a form: it took a record page's head and board, so its first field sits at 706px against the backend's 334px. A create or edit form needs the dense pattern — a one-row head, counts in a thin strip, the air between groups.

Before you hand in a screen DESIGN.md §40

A reskinned backend screen is done when every line holds, measured in the browser, not looked at.

  1. It is one of the five page types and starts from that type's reference page.
  2. Every component is a ui-* class from this catalogue; nothing is restyled in page CSS.
  3. Only the type roles above: no new size, weight, caps or font.
  4. Caps only on table head keys, spec keys and menu group labels; everything else in sentence case.
  5. One primary button per view; buttons radius 9; red only on Hidden on site and Clear all.
  6. Every radius from the scale, 0 where surfaces touch; one shadow per floating object; the sticky stack has one shadow at its bottom.
  7. Nothing moves on any state change: hover, select, open, save, error, loading. Shift measured at 0px.
  8. Nothing touches an edge: 8px or more inside every tag, chip, badge and menu item; 10px inside every button; 16px beside every divider.
  9. Table columns left-aligned; field labels on one line; a pager at the top and bottom of a paged list.
  10. No explanatory text on a dealer page; internal names and codes only in title.
  11. Empty, loading, error and long-content states shown in the browser.
  12. No horizontal overflow at 1280, 1366, 1440 and 1920, nor at 768, 834, 1024 and 1180; no console errors.
  13. On touch nothing waits for hover; every control 36px, or an invisible 36px hit area.
  14. Shown only side by side with its Gen 11 reference, and only when it matches.
