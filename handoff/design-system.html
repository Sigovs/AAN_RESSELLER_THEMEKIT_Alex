Red, twice only

Red marks Hidden on site and Clear all. A destructive action is danger text, never a red fill.

One means per state

Never colour alone

A tone always comes with its word. Status reads in words; red never grades a fact.

Weight, not size

Hierarchy comes from weight. No size outside these roles.

Square where surfaces touch

Radius 0 on every touching edge. A band inside a sheet has no radius of its own; the sheet keeps the corners.

Buttons are 9, always

No pill buttons, fields, facets or tab strips. The pill is a meaning: a state, a flag, a count.

Elevation

DESIGN.md §2 · §4

Four shadows and the sticky-stack edge. Depth says what floats; it is never decoration.

--sh-pillResting controlssheet buttons, facets, the command search
--sh-softFloating sheetsplatform bar, field sheet, record head, in-page popovers
--el-2Side panelthe dock
--el-3Overlaysbar menus, dialogs, overlay dock
stackSticky stack, engagedone shadow at the bottom of bar + band + column head

One shadow per floating object

Never two shadows on one boundary. Attached surfaces have none: they share a hairline.

The sticky stack casts one

Bar, band and column head engage as one surface with one shadow at the bottom edge. Interior shadows are removed, not softened.

Focus and motion

DESIGN.md §12 · §14

How the system shows where you are and how it changes state.

Focus on dark2px #ffd98a outline, offset 2

240 msdisclosure — --dur2
cubic-bezier(.2, 0, 0, 1)
One easing--ease, everywhere

Focus owns the outline

Only :focus-visible draws it; nothing draws focus with a shadow; nothing clips it.

Nothing jumps

Motion is for disclosure and state, never for arrival. No state change moves layout: space is reserved. Reduced motion lands in the same place instantly.

Components

Buttons

Origin: All Vehicles · DESIGN.md §11 · §38.1

One primary per view; every other action is secondary or ghost. Radius 9 on every button, sized by its label, 8px apart.

Variants × states

Sizes · drawn with Single Vehicle's CSS

Destructive · drawn with Manage Dealers's CSS

Do

  • One filled primary per view.
  • Sentence case, three words or fewer.
  • Padding 14 (small 10); text never touches the edge.
  • Icon-only: aria-label and title.

Don't

  • A second filled button, a pill, a red fill.
  • A button that changes width when its label changes.
  • Loading that hides the label: the button is disabled, the label stays.

Text fields and select

Origin: Single Vehicle · DESIGN.md §24 · §38.11

One value to type or pick. 36 tall, radius 7, a 1px edge; the label sits above, the help line under.

Text input

Select and textarea

Field: label, control, help · "?" mark

Missing value (Dealer Edit rates) · drawn with Dealer Edit's CSS

States on the pages

  • Focus: brand edge plus the 2px ring.
  • Disabled: wash fill, ink-3 text.
  • Empty read-only value: "not set" in ink-4, never blank.
  • Error: no error style exists yet; the result stands beside the action that produced it, 16px to its left (§11).

Don't

  • A placeholder instead of a label.
  • A label on two lines: widen the cell.
  • Mono for names, money or dates.

Status chips, tags and badges

Origin: All Vehicles · DESIGN.md §27 · §38.6–38.7

A record's state, a flag on a record, a count, provenance. Sentence case everywhere; at least 8px of air inside.

Do

  • Status chip: 24 tall, radius 7, its tone on a soft fill with a 1px ring.
  • The column is as wide as the longest state; a chip never truncates.
  • At most two flags, then +N.

Don't

  • Caps: "No price", never "NO PRICE".
  • A pill-shaped status chip, or a chip for a count.
  • Grades: "health", "needs work", a green "OK". Red only for Hidden on site.

Which one

  • Find within the list: search, one per list.
  • One dimension, few values: facet + popover.
  • Many dimensions: Filters, opening the dock.
  • Saved views of one list: view tabs.

Don't

  • A native select in the band.
  • A chosen tab that turns bold: it moves its neighbours.
  • Two rows of tabs; a tab that filters a feed.

Menus and popovers

Origin: All Vehicles · DESIGN.md §28 · §38.9 · §38.13

A popover opens under its trigger, never covers it, and closes on outside click and Esc.

In-page popover

Do

  • Item 34 tall, padding 8 10, radius 7, 14 / 400 ink.
  • The chosen item in brand-ink 600, with aria-current.
  • A long list gets a find field on top and Clear in a footer.

Don't

  • An icon on every item.
  • More than one level.
  • A popover that covers its own trigger.
